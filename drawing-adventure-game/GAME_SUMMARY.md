# Welcome to the World of Ink

**What if your art wasn't just something you made, but part of the world you explored?**

This is a 2D multiplayer adventure built around drawing, discovery, and the people you meet along the way. Set out from the Camp and descend into a huge network of caves, where strange places, valuable ores, and other players are waiting to be found.

## Draw your own identity

Your character doesn't have to look like everyone else's. Draw your own appearance, clothes, and artwork, then bring your creations to life with the colors you collect. Ink is more than decoration: it's part of how you fight, explore, and interact with the world.

## Keep going deeper

The caves start quiet, but the deeper you travel, the more the world can change. New creatures and merchants may appear, and rare biomes could make familiar tunnels feel completely different. The deeper cave details are still being developed, so there should always be more to discover. The journey should feel like a shared adventure, with other players appearing along the way rather than waiting for you in a lobby.

## Build a weapon that's yours

Weapons are made from three parts: a blade, a handle, and an amulet. Each part has a role, giving you room to find combinations that suit how you want to play. Collect ink and shards, improve your gear, and discover what works best for you.

## Take the fight to Inkoff

When you're ready for competition, Inkoff puts two teams against each other with two ways to win: collect and deliver gel to fill your team's base, or take all three lives from the opposing Queen. Workers race to carry gel home while the Queen brings a different kind of power to the fight. Every match can turn on teamwork, timing, and how you use your abilities.

## Make the world social

Exploration isn't just about what's around the next corner. You can meet other players, build friendships, and unlock gestures and paired actions, from simple greetings to more personal moments. The aim is to make encounters feel memorable, whether you travel together or just cross paths for a little while.

## A world still taking shape

The game is in development, and some systems are still being figured out. The big idea is already clear: **a world where drawing, exploration, combat, and social play all belong together.** Find something strange in the caves, make something that feels like you, and see who you meet along the way.
