# Project Overview _AI

> AI-drafted documentation based on the creator's latest clarifications and existing project notes. Creator decisions take precedence.

## High concept
A 2D multiplayer drawing adventure set on a large planet. Players explore a shared world, descend through cave layers, gather ink and shards, build weapons from interchangeable parts, and create artwork through an unusual glitch-based drawing system. The social experience emphasizes naturally encountering other players during exploration, seeing their custom character skins after initiating a handshake, and gradually unlocking more expressive interactions through friendship.

## Core pillars
1. Explore a large planet, begin at the Camp, and discover increasingly dangerous cave layers.
2. Create artwork on a persistent personal board and on a separate planet reached through a glitch artifact.
3. Collect ink and shards by exploring and mining ores.
4. Build weapons from a blade, handle, and amulet. The blade defines the primary attack, the handle defines the secondary ability, and the amulet provides passive advantages and defines a Queen's projectile pattern in Inkoff.
5. Encounter other players naturally during cave exploration and initiate handshakes to reveal one another's custom character skins.
6. Compete in Inkoff, with gel delivery and Queen elimination as victory routes.
7. Encounter strange merchants. Yap and Trap can appear in cave layers, with appearance chances increasing deeper underground.

## World structure
The original daily island, seasonal island, and white island plan is scrapped. The game is set on a large planet. The Camp is the starting area, a place to talk to certain characters, and the main location for accessing the player's stash, where weapons can be stored. The caves begin near the surface and descend through transitions that switch the player between server instances.

A rare cave-biome system can occasionally replace the usual cave system with a cave network that has special traits. This is a rare variation, not a set of ordinary seasonal islands.

## Confirmed removals
- No original island-hopping world structure.
- No white emergency ore in Inkoff.
- No colored shards; the currency is simply shards.
- No item unlock tokens in the current plan.
- Custom-drawn weapon skins are not currently planned and should not be treated as a confirmed feature.
- The earlier idea of transferring ink to teammates by slashing them is rejected. Teammate slashes do not transfer ink.

## Drawing and color interaction
Drawing is a core activity rather than a side menu. The current editor concept uses black and shades of gray for drawing; white is the canvas/background rather than a normal drawing ink. A player can drag an ink color onto a gray or filled region to recolor matching areas. The exact fill algorithm remains open, and the earlier idea of special gray “alloy” regions that can become any chosen color is only a candidate, not a confirmed rule.

## Important distinctions
- Ink and shards: players can carry effectively unlimited amounts; the stash is mainly for weapons and special artifacts.
- Weapon locking: a player assembles a weapon from a blade, handle, and amulet, then can pay shards to lock the combination. Locking enables use in modes such as Inkoff and allows upgrades. It prevents ordinary editing of the locked combination, except for changes made through Trap's service.
- Inkoff ink: every player starts with the same amount of ink. Players choose an available ink color and can mine ores during the match to replenish ink. If their ink runs out, the current stated rule is that they die; there is no white-ore fallback.
- Drawing: artwork ink consumption depends on how much of each color is used. Ink is expected to be common and visible throughout the world.
