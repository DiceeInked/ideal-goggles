# Project Overview

A 2D multiplayer drawing adventure set on a large planet. Players start at the Camp, explore caves, collect ink and shards, build weapons from parts, draw their own characters and artwork, and meet other players along the way.

## Main ideas
- Explore a large planet and descend into more dangerous cave layers.
- Draw character looks, clothes, and art on a persistent personal board and a separate drawing planet.
- Collect ink and shards by exploring and mining.
- Build weapons from a blade, handle, and amulet. The blade controls the main attack, the handle controls the secondary ability, and the amulet gives passive benefits and sets the Queen's projectile pattern in Inkoff.
- Meet other players naturally while exploring and use friendship actions to interact.
- Play Inkoff, where teams win by filling their base with gel or taking all three of the opposing Queen's lives.
- Meet merchants such as YAP and Trap in deeper cave layers.

## World
The old daily, seasonal, and white-island plan is scrapped. The game takes place on one large planet, with the Camp as the starting area. Caves descend through hidden passages and server transitions. Rare cave biomes may replace the usual cave network, but their effects aren't decided.

## Drawing
The drawing editor starts with black and shades of gray; white is the canvas. Dragging ink onto a gray or filled area recolors matching regions. The exact fill rules are still being designed. A special gray “alloy” that can become any ink color is only an idea for now.

A small glitchy portal at the Camp opens a personal drawing board that doesn't reset. A square glitch artifact can open a temporary portal to the player's stash. At the surface, it can also be used with a large antenna to reach a separate drawing planet. The privacy rules for that planet are undecided.

## Weapons, ink, and shards
Weapons have a blade, handle, and amulet. Players spend shards to lock a combination, which then can be upgraded and used in modes such as Inkoff. Locked weapons can't normally be edited, but Trap can attempt to split one apart through his challenge.

Ink is used for combat and art. It's common throughout the world, and art uses ink based on how much of each color is drawn. Players can carry effectively unlimited ink and shards; the stash is mainly for weapons and special artifacts.

Red is meant to help with healing or survivability, blue increases damage, and green improves luck or rewards. Yellow's effect isn't decided.

## Inkoff
Everyone starts with the same amount of ink and can mine ores for more. Running out of ink causes death. Workers respawn; the Queen loses one of three lives. A team wins by filling all the holes in its base with gel or by taking all three of the other Queen's lives. There is no white-ore fallback, and teammates don't transfer ink by slashing.

## Friends and social actions
Players can encounter one another naturally while exploring. A handshake can reveal a player's custom character skin. Friendship unlocks actions such as holding hands, guiding, chatting, piggybacking, and special greetings. The social-action menu is called **Passions**. The optional romantic-partner path requires both players to agree, and each player can have one partner at a time.

## Removed ideas
- Daily, seasonal, and white islands as the main world structure.
- White emergency ore in Inkoff.
- Colored shards and item unlock tokens.
- Giving teammates ink by slashing them.
- The old core-stone Inkoff win condition.
- Custom-drawn weapon skins.
