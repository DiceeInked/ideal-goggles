## Source-of-truth rules
- The newest decisions stated by the creator override older notes.
- Confirmed details are documented as current rules; unresolved matters are labelled Open question rather than guessed.
- AI-written documentation uses the _AI suffix.
- The former daily/seasonal island plan is scrapped. The game takes place on a large planet, with the Camp as the starting area and the caves as a major exploration route.
- White emergency ore, colored shards, unlock tokens, and custom-drawn weapon skins are not part of the current plan.
