# Social Play and Friendship Actions _AI

> AI-drafted documentation based on the creator's latest clarifications.

## Natural encounters
Players can encounter other players while exploring caves. Other players may appear and pass by, helping the world feel shared rather than like a sequence of isolated solo levels. The inspiration is the social-travel feeling of Sky: Children of the Light, adapted to this game's own 2D planet and cave systems.

## Showing a player's custom skin
Players can initiate a handshake. The handshake then reveals/views the other player's custom character skin. The exact default silhouette or concealment presentation before the handshake is not yet documented.

## Friendship progression
After starting a friendship with another character, players can spend certain points to increase intimacy/progression. Eventually, a player can choose a branch or path that unlocks different types of actions:
- friendship-related actions,
- romantic-themed actions,
- other action categories.

The system may allow players to mix or replace greeting actions with alternatives such as a hug or kiss. Piggybacking is another possible social action inspired by Sky. This is a design description of unlockable in-game emotes/actions, not a finalized progression tree.

## Ink and helpful interactions
The earlier idea that a player could slash a teammate with a weapon to transfer ink has been rejected. Slashing a teammate does not give them ink.
