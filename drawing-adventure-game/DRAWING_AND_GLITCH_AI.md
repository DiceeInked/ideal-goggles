# Drawing, Glitch Artifact, and Stash Portal _AI

> AI-drafted documentation based on the creator's latest clarifications.

## Ink in artwork
The amount of ink consumed by artwork depends on how much of each color the player uses. Ink is expected to be plentiful and visibly available throughout the world, rather than a scarce premium resource for ordinary drawing.

The older proposal for daily and seasonal islands as the primary color sources is scrapped.

## Persistent personal board at Camp
At the Camp, a small glitchy portal lets the player access a personal drawing board. This board does not reset. It is the player's own persistent space for drawing whatever they want.

This Camp board is distinct from the separate planet accessed through the glitch artifact.

## Finding the glitch artifact
After the player has mined and explored to a modest depth, around the second or third cave level, they can discover a glitchy, square-shaped object. It may also be discoverable during the tutorial; that placement is still being considered.

The artifact enables a remote stash portal mechanic.

## Opening a remote stash portal
1. The player touches and holds their chest for about five seconds.
2. During the hold, the character visibly begins panting.
3. A glitch effect appears at the player's mouse cursor.
4. The player drives the cursor into a wall to open a portal.
5. The portal remains open for about 30 seconds, unless the player opens another portal first, which closes the previous one.
6. The player can throw items through the portal. Items sent through are immediately placed into the player's stash.

This is mainly for weapon parts and special artifacts. Ink and shards do not need to be stored because players can carry effectively unlimited amounts.

## Using the artifact at the surface
At the surface, the player can use the glitch artifact on something that looks like a large antenna. The player can then jump into the opening and be teleported to a separate planet.

On that planet, players can walk around and use boards or similar surfaces to draw. This is a separate drawing environment from the persistent personal board available through the small Camp portal.
