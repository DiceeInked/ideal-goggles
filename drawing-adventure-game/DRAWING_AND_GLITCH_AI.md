# Drawing, Glitch Artifact, Rift, and Boards

## Drawing and color

Drawing is a major part of the game. Players can make their own character looks, clothes, and artwork.

- The editor uses black and shades of gray to start. White is the canvas, not a normal ink color.
- Drag an ink color onto a gray or filled region to recolor matching areas. For example, red ink can turn a gray circle red.
- The fill system must prevent players from joining separate areas together to recolor more than intended. The exact rule still needs designing.
- Artwork uses ink based on how much of each color is used. Ink should be common enough that players don't have to worry too much about drawing.
- A special gray “alloy” color that can become any chosen ink has been discussed, but isn't confirmed.

The old plan of using daily and seasonal islands as color sources is scrapped.

## Personal drawing board at the Camp

A small, physical drawing board at the Camp is persistent: drawings saved there do not reset. Players can draw on the Camp itself, and other players can see those drawings. The small personal board is distinct from the shared boards accessed from the antenna planet.

## Finding the glitch artifact

Older notes say a square, glitchy artifact may be found around the second or third cave level, or might appear as part of the tutorial. Its final placement is not yet decided.

## Rift and portable stash portal

The latest filled answer calls the portable stash portal the **Rift**. Players can leave a cave at any time by jumping into the Rift. This is a one-way exit from that cave trip and returns the player to the surface/Camp. The Rift can also send items directly to the player's stash.

The earlier portal sequence describes:
1. Hold the character's chest for about five seconds. The character starts panting.
2. A glitch effect appears at the cursor.
3. Move the cursor into a wall to open a portal.
4. The portal stays open for about 30 seconds, unless another portal is opened first.
5. Throw items through the portal to send them to the player's stash.

The stash is mainly for weapons and special artifacts. Players can carry effectively unlimited ink and shards, so those do not need to be stored.

## The Grid and shared boards

The latest notes describe **The Grid** as a small, round neon-circle planet that players can walk around. A merchant stands at the top, with antennas representing boards the player has joined. Talking to the merchant allows the player to join other boards and worlds. The merchant should feel mysterious, knowledgeable, and boastful about its capabilities.

The Grid is described as the access point for multiplayer features, including Inkoff and shared boards. Its relationship to the main cave planet is not yet clear.
