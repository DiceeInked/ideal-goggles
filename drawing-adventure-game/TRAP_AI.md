# Trap _AI

> AI-drafted merchant reference. The original creator-written notes remain in MERCHANTS.md.

## Appearance
Trap is a merchant. The creator's original note describes him as having a bright smile with sharp teeth and a sticky note over his left eye marked with a target.

## Core service
Trap offers a risky service that can take apart a locked weapon if the player wins his challenge. This is important because a locked weapon normally cannot be edited. Trap's service is the exception that can allow the player to change a locked combination.

## Why the challenge has two successful stages
The weapon is clamped down on two sides: one clamp to the left of the handle and one to the right. The amulet is displayed on one side of the handle, and the other weapon component is displayed on the other side. Trap's machine presents the mechanism as a revolver. A correct guess causes it to fire and split the weapon precisely down the middle. The player must successfully complete the challenge twice, splitting the weapon twice so the original three components can be separated. The two challenge stages are intentional, even if repeating the challenge may feel annoying.

## Challenge sequence
The original creator-authored notes describe the challenge as follows:
1. The player places a locked weapon into Trap's machine, locks it down, signs a contract, and begins.
2. The machine clamps the weapon on the left and right sides of the handle.
3. A barrel spins and shows five numbers one after another in mysterious blue static light, each visible for 0.5 seconds. A notepad is available to write them down.
4. The player selects one of three buttons: respin (costs 10 ink), guess the top barrel, or guess the bottom barrel.
5. The puzzle's stated trick is to move clockwise by one number and counterclockwise from a position, and so on. The exact instruction wording is ambiguous in the original source note and should be clarified before implementation.
6. The player has 60 seconds to answer. A revolver indicator advances one tick every 10 seconds, five times, then fires and kills the player.
7. A correct guess splits the weapon down the middle. The player must win twice to split the weapon into its three separate components.

The source note says it costs 2 shards to play.

## Relationship to weapon locking
A locked weapon's blade, handle, and amulet combination is ordinarily immutable. Trap's service is the explicit exception. The two-sided clamp and two successful splitting stages explain why the challenge is performed twice.

## Cave appearance
Trap can appear in deeper cave layers, and merchant appearance chances increase with depth. The initial cave has no shopkeepers.
