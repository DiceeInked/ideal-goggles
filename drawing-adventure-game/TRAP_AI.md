# Trap _AI

> AI-drafted merchant reference. The original creator-written notes remain in MERCHANTS.md.

## Appearance
Trap is a merchant. The creator's original note describes him as having a bright smile with sharp teeth and a sticky note over his left eye marked with a target.

## Core service
Trap offers a risky service that can take apart a locked weapon if the player wins his challenge. This is important because a locked weapon normally cannot be edited. Trap's service is the exception that can allow the player to change a locked combination.

The original creator-authored notes describe the challenge as follows:
1. The player places a locked weapon into Trap's machine, locks it down, signs a contract, and begins.
2. The player takes the two parts where the weapon is locked down and looks at one of them.
3. A barrel spins and shows five numbers one after another in mysterious blue static light, each visible for 0.5 seconds. A notepad is available to write them down.
4. The player then selects one of three buttons: respin (costs 10 ink), guess the top barrel, or guess the bottom barrel.
5. The puzzle's stated trick is to move clockwise by one number and counterclockwise from a position, and so on. The exact instruction wording is ambiguous in the source note and should be clarified before implementation.
6. The player has 60 seconds to answer. A revolver indicator advances one tick every 10 seconds, five times, then fires and kills the player.
7. If the player wins twice, the item is split into three.

The source note says it costs 2 shards to play.

## Relationship to weapon locking
A locked weapon's blade, handle, and amulet combination is ordinarily immutable. Trap's service is the explicit exception. The precise outcome of splitting the item into three should be checked against the current three-component weapon system; the original note says two parts in the challenge setup but later says the item splits into three. This inconsistency is preserved as an open question, not silently resolved.

## Cave appearance
Trap can appear in deeper cave layers, and merchant appearance chances increase with depth. The initial cave has no shopkeepers.
