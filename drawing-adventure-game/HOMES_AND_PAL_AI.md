# Homes, Props, Badges, and PAL

These ideas appeared in earlier notes, but their place in the current planet-and-Camp design needs to be confirmed.

## Player homes
Each player was meant to have a personal home that also served as their normal spawn. Players could draw on it, place props and badges, and show their art and achievements to visitors.

It's not yet decided whether homes are still planned, where players would access them, or how privacy and visits would work.

## Passionate Art Lineup (PAL)
PAL stands for **Passionate Art Lineup**. The original idea was a seasonal and yearly art event where players submit their own art. Selected winners' work would be offered for purchase and could be displayed in a player's home. Opening the display would show the artwork, title, and artist's name.

Earlier notes also proposed that artists receive in-game currency when their work sells. The winners, voting or judging process, prices, payout, and length of time each work is available are undecided.

## Still undecided
- Are homes still part of the game, and where do they fit with the Camp?
- Is PAL still planned?
- What currency do artists earn from sales?
- What rules and moderation tools are needed before art is shared publicly?
