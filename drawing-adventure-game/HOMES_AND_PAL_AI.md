## Player homes
Earlier design notes describe each player having a personal home that serves as their normal spawn location. Players can draw/customize the home and decorate it with placeable props and badges. Homes can act as personal galleries where visitors see a player's artwork and accomplishments.

The exact visitor permissions, privacy controls, and whether the home remains the normal spawn location in the revised planet-and-Camp structure are open questions. The new Camp/stash setup may change how homes connect to spawning.

## Passionate Art Lineup (PAL)
PAL stands for Passionate Art Lineup. Earlier notes describe a seasonal and yearly community-art submission event. Players submit original art, and selected winning works are featured for other players to purchase. Buyers receive a displayable version for their home, and selecting it opens a full-screen view with the artwork, title, and artist attribution.

The earlier notes say creators receive in-game currency when their art is purchased, but the currency payout and sale rules need confirmation. The exact number of winners, submission rules, judging/voting method, pricing, creator payout, and how long works remain available are open questions.
