# Passions _AI

> AI-drafted reference for social actions and emotes. Costs and thresholds are not finalized.

## Name
**Passions** is the chosen name for the game's friendship actions/emotes system.

## Heart Points
**Heart Points** is the working name for a separate currency used only for friendship-related actions and unlocks. Players can earn it by helping others, including healing them or performing other beneficial actions. Exact rewards and qualifying actions are undecided. Heart Points are separate from shards.

The earlier idea of sending shard pieces as a daily friendship gift is no longer the preferred plan. The replacement gift mechanic is still undecided. Direct gifts require the recipient's acceptance.

## General friendship actions
Potential actions and abilities include handshake (which can reveal another player's custom skin), holding hands, guiding a friend through the realms, chatting, piggybacking, custom greetings, and other emotes. These can unlock through the ordinary friendship tree.

## Romantic-partner path
At a later stage, players may choose the optional side branch labelled **“Make this a romantic partner.”** Both players must accept, and a player can have only one romantic partner at a time.

Unlocking the partnership immediately allows the players to add a special greeting to their greeting choices. The greeting can offer a small selection of actions; the exact initial options are not finalized.

Proposed gesture progression:
1. **Special bow animation:** available immediately when the romantic-partner path unlocks.
2. **Hand-to-heart gesture:** unlocked later and purchased with Heart Points.
3. **Hug gesture:** unlocked later and purchased with Heart Points.
4. **Kiss gesture:** a later unlock after the earlier gestures, also proposed to use Heart Points.

The exact order, costs, and friendship levels remain undecided. Romantic status grants no combat advantage or extra Inkoff resources.

## Consent and customization
Social actions should be opt-in. Players need a way to accept or decline a requested interaction, including romantic gestures, and the ability to end a partnership. Exact controls and privacy settings are open questions.
