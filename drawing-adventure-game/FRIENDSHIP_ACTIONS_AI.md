# Passions: Gestures and Paired Actions

**Passions** is the name of the game's social-action and emote system. Exact costs, friendship levels, and controls are still being worked out.

## Heart Points and gifts
**Heart Points** is the current working name for a friendship currency, separate from shards. Players may earn it by helping others, such as healing them, but the rewards aren't decided yet.

Sending shard pieces as a daily gift is no longer the preferred idea. The replacement gift system is undecided, and the recipient must accept a direct gift.

## Friendship actions
Possible unlocks include handshakes, holding hands, guiding a friend, chatting, piggybacking, custom greetings, and other gestures. A handshake can reveal another player's custom character skin.

## Passions menu and paired actions
Players can choose a special greeting in the settings. When they meet their romantic partner, they can start that greeting to show each other's custom character skins.

For a paired gesture, one player starts it from the Passions menu. The other player confirms by approaching and pressing the relevant button. There is no separate accept/decline pop-up; not approaching and confirming means the gesture doesn't happen.

Players may be able to choose a gesture and a short flourish for their greeting. The menu and controls aren't final.

## Romantic-partner path
The romantic-partner option is a short, optional branch of the friendship tree. Both players must agree to the proposal or upgrade. Each player can have one romantic partner at a time and any number of ordinary friends.

Becoming partners unlocks the special greeting right away. The planned gesture order is:
1. **Special bow:** unlocked first.
2. **Hand-to-heart:** unlocked later.
3. **Hug:** unlocked later.
4. **Kiss:** unlocked after the others.

The order is planned, but the friendship levels and costs aren't decided. Heart Points might be used for later gestures, but that isn't confirmed.

## Gesture animation ideas
These are concepts, not final animation specs.

- **Special bow:** a distinct bowing pose, with no extra particles.
- **Hand-to-heart:** the character places a hand over their chest; a hand-drawn heart pulses once.
- **Hug:** both characters move into an embrace. A few ink droplets orbit them once, using both players' ink colors.
- **Kiss:** the characters lean together. A heart-shaped ink mark appears between their faces, floats upward, and fades.

Nearby players can see the effects. Their colors should blend the two players' inks. Effects may become more noticeable as friendship grows, but the amount isn't decided.

Characters use separate head, arm, leg, and torso parts, with outfits attached to those parts. Gestures should animate these parts so the clothing follows naturally. Paired gestures need enough room around the players; hugs and kisses require them to stand close together first.

The bow may last about one second and the kiss about four seconds. These are rough ideas, not final timings. Walking away may cancel some gestures, but the rules aren't decided. There should also be a special response if someone disconnects during a gesture, though the exact animation is still open.

## Ending a partnership
Either partner can end the partnership after a confirmation prompt. The other partner's related gestures and friendship-tree upgrades stay visible but become greyed out and unusable. The rules for starting a new partnership after a breakup aren't set yet.

## Health when close
When two players reach the friendship level where the partner option unlocks, both slowly regain health while nearby, whether or not they become partners. A heart appears on each player's chest and beats while they are close.

The healing rate and distance aren't decided. Romantic-partner status itself does not affect Inkoff, but it's still undecided whether this close-friend health effect works in that mode.
