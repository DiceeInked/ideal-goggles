## Scribble Spider
**Creature level:** 3.

The Scribble Spider is incredibly dangerous and fidgety. It runs away when damaged too much, so killing it is not recommended. It can hang from almost anywhere as long as it is holding on with one limb. It stalks the player and has a strong horror feel.
