# Creatures and Cave Encounters

The first cave area has a few ores but no enemies or shopkeepers. More types of creatures can appear as players go deeper.

Rare cave biomes may replace the usual cave network with a special one. It's not yet decided whether these biomes will have unique creatures, hazards, ores, movement rules, or other effects.

## Still undecided
- Creature names and behaviors.
- Which creatures appear at each depth and how often.
- Whether any creatures are friendly, neutral, or interactable.
- Whether special biomes have their own creatures.
- What happens to difficulty if players return to an earlier cave layer.
