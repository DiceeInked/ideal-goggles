# Merchant Reference

This file is the current concise reference for merchants. For specific rules, the creator's latest explicit clarification comes first, then the uploaded PDF, then older repository notes.

Merchants are special characters who offer items or services. The original idea says the merchants know one another, though it is not clear whether they want to talk about it. The first cave has no shopkeepers, and merchants become more likely the deeper players travel.

## YAP

### Appearance
YAP has a plus sign over his right eye and a sticky note marked with an X over his left eye.

### Four shop items
YAP sells:
1. A random blade part, without buffs or tonics.
2. A random handle part, without buffs or tonics.
3. A random amulet part, without buffs or tonics.
4. A random completed weapon. Some completed weapons may have a tonic, which is a buff. Tonics can apply to the completed weapon; the separate blade, handle, and amulet items cannot have buffs.

### Summoning and spawn depth
The uploaded PDF says YAP appears at A1 and lower. The player can tip him with any color of ink to attempt to summon him; each tip has a 10% chance to spawn him. Items are purchased with shards.

## Trap

### Appearance
Trap has a bright smile with sharp teeth and a sticky note on his left eye marked with a target.

### Service
Trap can attempt to separate a locked weapon into its three parts. The player places a locked weapon into his machine, clamps it down, signs a contract, and starts a challenge. Winning twice separates the weapon into three parts. The service costs 2 shards.

### Current challenge rules from the PDF
- A six-slot barrel is shown with a glowing marker in the top slot.
- Five numbers appear one after another in a light-blue glowing font.
- The intended answer rule is to move the first number clockwise from the top, then the next number counterclockwise from that position, and continue alternating.
- The revolver points at the player and spins for 10 seconds, then fires every three seconds with a one-in-six chance to kill the player.
- An older repository note says respinning costs 10 ink; this remains the working cost because the PDF does not contradict it.

The older repository draft's 60-second deadline, five ticks spaced ten seconds apart, and guaranteed fatal shot are superseded by the PDF.
