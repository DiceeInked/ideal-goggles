# Merchants
> Merchants are special characters that you can communicate with to get items and they all know each other! I’m not sure if they actually want to talk about that.

## Yap
> A merchant that has a + on their right eye and a sticky note with an x on their left eye.

### Sells:
- One locked weapon with a special tonic(see TONICS) on the entire thing.
- One blade part.
- One handle part.
- One amulet part.

### Accepts:
- Any color of ink to initially summon them.
- Shards for buying items.


## Trap
> A merchant that has a bright smile with sharp teeth and a sticky note on their left eye with a target on it.

### Sells:
- His ability to take apart items, that is if you win.
